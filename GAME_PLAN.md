Food Log app is going to be a way for users to track their calories. 


This app will have three view to start.

Home View, Food Catalog View, and Summary/Stat view

Home View:
    The top of this view will display either 'Today' or a future or past date. This will allow have buttons to go forward and backward
    below the date will be the main focus of the diet (for example Calories) and any other macronutrients that the user is interested in tracking.
    Below this will be a list of all foods added for the given day.
    Toward the bottom of the screen will be a scaffold that allows the user to switch views or Add Food Items
