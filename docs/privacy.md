# Nutriton Privacy Policy

**Effective:** November 7, 2026

Nutriton is a calorie and macro tracker made by Andrew Cruz, an individual developer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## The short version

- Your food log, goals, body weight and settings are stored **only on your device**.
- Nutriton has **no account**, **no ads**, **no analytics** and **no tracking**.
- Nothing is sent to the developer. The app talks to the internet only for the three things listed below.

## What stays on your device

Everything you enter, including foods, meals, recipes, your log, goals, body weight readings, reminders and settings, is saved in a database on your phone. The developer cannot see it.

Your device's own backup may include this data: iCloud Backup on iPhone, or Google's Android backup on Android. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under your account and their privacy policies.

The app also keeps a short list of recent errors on the device, which you can view in Settings. It is never sent anywhere unless you choose to share it.

## When the app uses the internet

1. **Food lookups (Open Food Facts).** When you scan a barcode or search for a food online, the barcode number or the search text is sent to [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org), a free, public food database, to fetch nutrition facts. Nothing else about you or your log is sent. Open Food Facts' handling of requests is described in [its privacy policy](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy).
2. **Purchases (Apple, Google and RevenueCat).** If you buy Nutriton Pro, start the trial or leave a tip, the purchase is processed by the App Store or Google Play. The app uses [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) to check which purchases you own. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created by the app and your purchase history for Nutriton, not your name, email or log. The developer can see that an anonymous user bought Pro, but not who.
3. **Opening links.** Links you tap, such as this policy, open in your browser.

## Apple Health and Health Connect

If you turn on the Apple Health options (a Pro feature), Nutriton writes the nutrition totals you log (energy, protein, carbohydrates, fat, fiber, sugar and sodium) to Apple Health and reads your body weight from it. On Android, Health Connect would work the same way.

This health data moves only between Nutriton and Health on your device. It is never sent to the developer or any other party, and it is never used for advertising or marketing. You can turn the options off, or revoke access in the Health app or Health Connect settings, at any time.

## Notifications and the home screen widget

Reminders are scheduled on your device. The home screen widget shows today's totals by reading them from the app on your device. Neither sends data anywhere.

## Sharing and exporting

Backups and exports are files you create and choose where to send. Once you share a file, the app you share it with handles it.

## Children

Nutriton is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Your choices

Because your data is on your device, you control it directly: edit or delete entries in the app, export a backup in Settings, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on the device. There is nothing held by the developer to request or delete. Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as required to restore purchases.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. A change that sends more data off your device would be described in the app before it takes effect.

## Contact
